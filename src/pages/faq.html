What is MyDearTeenager?expand_more

A learning platform for 13 to 18 year olds. You pick a skill, learn it through short lessons, practise with quick checks, make a real project, and add it to your portfolio. Then you can find competitions, scholarships and other opportunities to use it.

When does MDT launch?expand_more

We're building it now. Everyone on the waitlist hears the launch date first.

What can I learn?expand_more

Our first paths cover design, coding, content creation, entrepreneurship, digital marketing, money, AI tools, public speaking and personal growth. Browse the Skill Academy.

Do I get a certificate?expand_more

Yes, on eligible paths, once you've finished every lesson, check and project. It shows up in your portfolio.

What do I need to use it?expand_more

A phone, tablet or computer with a web browser and an internet connection. There's nothing to install.

I'm 12. Can I join?expand_more

Not yet. MDT is for ages 13 to 18. Ask a parent to join the waitlist for you and we'll be here when you're ready.

Does MDT replace school?expand_more

No. MDT sits alongside school and adds practical skills that school doesn't always have time for.

How does parental consent work?expand_more

When a teen under 18 signs up, we email their parent or guardian. Nothing is kept until you confirm. You can change your mind at any time. Read more for parents.

Can I see my child's progress?expand_more

Your child's portfolio shows the projects they've finished. A dedicated parent view is on our roadmap for a later release.

How much time should my child spend on MDT?expand_more

Little and often works best. 15 to 30 minutes a few times a week adds up quickly.

Is MDT safe?expand_more

Safety is built in: parental consent for under-18s, a moderated community with clear rules, a way to report anything, and safeguarding checks for tutors. See how we keep MDT safe.

What data do you collect?expand_more

Only what we need to run your account and your learning. Our privacy policy explains exactly what and why.

How do I report something?expand_more

Use the report button on any post, comment, message or profile. A moderator will review it. You can also write to safety@yourdomain.

Can I delete my account?expand_more

Yes. You, or your parent or guardian, can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

How do I become a tutor?expand_more

Apply on our teach page. We'll have a chat, run a safeguarding check and help you plan your path.
