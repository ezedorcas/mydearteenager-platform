Dear parent,

You've probably watched your child disappear into a phone and wondered what it's doing to them. We've wondered the same thing.

Teenagers are some of the most creative, energetic people in any community. They're also growing up with more pressure from more directions than ever: peers, social media, and a world that changes faster than any curriculum can keep up with.

We don't think the answer is to take the phone away. We think it's to give that time somewhere better to go. A place where your child learns something useful, makes something real, and gets recognised for showing up.

That's MyDearTeenager. It sits alongside school, not against it. It's built for 13 to 18 year olds, and nobody under 18 joins without a parent or guardian saying yes.

We'd love you to be part of it.

Warmly,
The MyDearTeenager team

How consent works.

Nigerian data protection law says we need a parent or guardian's consent before we keep a child's details. Here's how that will work on MDT.

  1. Your teen signs up

    They give a first name, their age and your email address. Nothing else yet.

  2. You get an email

    It explains what MDT is, what we'll store and why.

  3. You say yes, or no

    One tap to confirm. If you say no, or don't reply, we delete what they entered.

  4. They start learning

    You can change your mind and ask us to close the account at any time.
