1. Who can use MDT

Learners must be aged 13 to 18. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms and confirm your account. Tutors must be adults and pass our safeguarding check.

2. Your account

Keep your password private and don't share your account. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it. Give us accurate information when you sign up.

3. Community rules

Everyone on MDT follows our community guidelines. We may remove content or accounts that break them.

4. Your work

Projects you make belong to you. By posting them on MDT, you let us show them in your portfolio and to the people you choose to share them with. confirm licence wording

5. Tutors and their content

Course content belongs to MDT or the tutor who created it, as agreed in the tutor agreement. You may use it for your own learning but not copy or resell it. align with tutor agreement

6. What's not allowed

  • Bullying, harassment, hate speech or threats.
  • Sharing anyone's personal details.
  • Posting work that isn't yours as if it were.
  • Trying to break, overload or misuse the service.
  • Using MDT to sell things or recruit people without our permission.

7. Ending your account

You can close your account at any time. We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms or put others at risk.

8. Our responsibilities

We work hard to keep MDT running and accurate, but we can't promise it will always be available or error-free. Opportunities listed by other organisations are their responsibility. limitation of liability clause
