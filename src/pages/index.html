Dear teenager,

We know you spend a lot of time on your phone. We're not here to lecture you about it.

The same phone you scroll on can design a poster, edit a short film, build a website or test a business idea. People your age are doing it right now. Most of them just needed someone to show them where to start.

So we're building that place. Short lessons, real projects, somewhere to keep your work, and people who want to see you win.

You don't have to know what you want to be yet. Start with what you're curious about. We'll figure out the rest together.

Rooting for you,

Is it safe for my child?expand_more

Safety is part of the design from the start. Under-18s need a parent or guardian to confirm their account, the community is moderated with clear rules, and anyone can report something that feels wrong. Read how we keep MDT safe.

Does it replace school?expand_more

No. MDT sits alongside school and adds the practical skills school doesn't always have time for.
