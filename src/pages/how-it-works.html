The loop every skill follows.

Watching isn't learning. Each path takes you from the idea to something you made, then shows you where to take it.

  1. route

    Learn

    Short video lessons, readings and demos, one idea at a time and in a set order, so you always know what's next.

    Video lessonsReadingsDownloads
  2. bolt

    Practise

    Quick checks and small assignments as you go. Get something wrong, try again, no drama.

    QuizzesAssignmentsReflections
  3. palette

    Make

    Every path ends with a project you build yourself. Where it fits, a tutor reviews it and gives you useful feedback.

    ProjectsTutor feedback
