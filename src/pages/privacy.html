1. Who we are

MyDearTeenager ("MDT", "we", "us") is operated by registered company name, registered in Nigeria under number RC number, at registered address. We are the data controller for the personal information described here.

2. What we collect

  • Waitlist: first name, email, role, age (for teens), a parent or guardian's email (for under-18s) and optional interests.
  • Accounts (after launch): name, age, country, learning interests, optional profile photo and optional gender.
  • Learning activity: lessons completed, quiz results, projects, XP, badges and certificates.
  • Community: posts, comments and reports you make.
  • Technical: device and browser information needed to keep the service working. confirm analytics and cookies used

3. Children and parental consent

MDT is for people aged 13 to 18. Under Nigerian law, we need the consent of a parent or legal guardian before we process the personal information of anyone under 18. When a teen signs up, we email their parent or guardian and keep only what's needed to ask for consent. If consent isn't given within number of days, we delete it.

We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

4. How we use it

  • To run your account and save your learning progress.
  • To show your projects, badges and certificates in your portfolio.
  • To suggest learning paths and opportunities that suit you.
  • To keep the community safe and respond to reports.
  • To send launch news and important account messages. You can unsubscribe from non-essential emails at any time.

We do not sell personal information. confirm: advertising policy

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run MDT (for example hosting and email), under contracts that protect it, and where the law requires. Tutors see the progress and projects of learners in their own courses. list processors and any transfers outside Nigeria

6. How long we keep it

7. Your rights

You (or your parent or guardian, if you're under 18) can ask to see the information we hold, correct it, delete it, or withdraw consent. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
