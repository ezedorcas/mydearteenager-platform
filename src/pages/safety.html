Community guidelines.

Every MDT member agrees to these before joining the community. Breaking them can mean losing access.

Short version: be the kind of person you'd want to learn next to.
  1. Be kind

    Give feedback you'd be happy to receive. Disagree with ideas, not people.

  2. Keep personal details private

    Don't share your address, phone number, school or passwords, or anyone else's.

  3. No bullying, hate or harassment

    Not in posts, comments, messages or projects. Not as a joke either.

  4. Credit other people's work

    Only post work you made, or say clearly where it came from.

  5. Keep it age-appropriate

    Nothing you wouldn't show a younger cousin.

  6. Speak up

    If something feels off, report it. You won't get in trouble for reporting.

What happens when you report something.

  1. You report it

    Tap report on any post, comment, message or profile, and say what's wrong.

  2. A moderator reviews it

    A real person looks at it. Serious reports go to the front of the queue.

  3. We act

    That might mean removing content, a warning, or removing someone from MDT.

  4. We follow up

    We let you know it's been handled. Your report stays confidential.
