Why we started,

Teenagers are some of the most vibrant people in any community. They're also growing up in a world that's gone completely digital, where people learn, work and make money in ways that didn't exist ten years ago.

That world can do real harm. Endless feeds, peer pressure, and too few people showing young people a better way to spend their time. Here in Nigeria, and across Africa, we see bright teenagers finish school without the practical skills or the confidence to go after what's out there.

We think it's worth investing in them early. Not by taking the phone away, but by making the time they already spend on it count for something.

So we're building MyDearTeenager: a companion that helps teens find a skill, learn it properly, make something real with it, and find opportunities to take it further.
